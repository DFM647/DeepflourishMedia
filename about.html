FOUNDER · DFM
Founder story

Driven by Purpose.
Built on Independence.

Founded by Taoheed, Deepflourish Media was born out of a critical gap in the modern music industry: artists were spending years creating profound, healing, and inspiring music, only to remain completely invisible on major search engines and broadcast networks.

In strategic partnership with Breccan Jorvik, we build the backend open-database registries, Schema JSON-LD code, and technical mastering required to help songs reach TV, film and official Google Knowledge Panel ecosystems—organically and on the artist's own terms.

What we stand for

Three principles behind DFM.

◈

100% Artist Ownership

We work with open, universal music databases and structured metadata so the artist retains ownership of their identity and information.

✦

Zero Artificial Manipulation

No bot plays, fake repost networks or vanity metrics. The focus is genuine, long-term organic discoverability.

◉

Broadcast Technical Excellence

We align sound delivery with professional loudness and True-Peak considerations without compromising the artist's creative vision.
